thesis_neoma_2024.pdf




MSc Marketing & Data Analytics  NEOMA  2023–2024  supervised by Laura Trinchera


Loyalty in the streaming era: what keeps subscribers on Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+


How much do SVoD loyalty strategies (personalization, exclusives, service quality, price, social proof) actually shape subscriber behavior in a crowded market?


214valid responses (of 284)
79%subscribe to 2+ platforms
78.9%log in daily or several times a week
5hypotheses, tested per segment










The five hypotheses


  • ◐Partially
    H1. Personalization drives loyaltySignificant only for multi-platform users, who like discovering new content.
  • ✓Confirmed
    H2. Exclusive content drives loyaltyExplains 27.6% of loyalty variance (single) and 20.7% (multi).
  • ◐Partially
    H3. UX/UI quality drives loyaltyStrong on satisfaction, not decisive on loyalty alone.
  • ✓Confirmed
    H4. Price influences loyalty60%+ rate it important. Top reason to switch for 44.4% of single-platform users.
  • ✕Rejected
    H5. Social recommendations matterRanked among the least important factors by both groups.


So what


Two profiles: single-platform users are price-first, multi-platform users (the majority) want service quality and variety. Retention needs a mix: flawless streaming, smart pricing tiers, steady exclusives, and segment-specific plays.


Method: Qualtrics survey  5-point Likert scales  correlations and linear regressions in SPSS and Excel

Master in Marketing  INSEEC  2020–2021  with Juliette Leroy  supervised by Manuel Lesaicherre


Big data in companies and its influence on trust inside smart buildings


How does the way companies use big data shape people's trust relationships within smart buildings?


2voices: subject-matter experts and building occupants
5variables studied
2kinds of trust: organizational and interpersonal


The model we explored



Key finding


It is not big data, or collecting it, that damages trust. It is the choices companies make about how they collect and, above all, use it.


Those choices decide whether a climate of trust emerges, and with it the level of well-being at work. Data strategy is a people strategy.


Method: qualitative study, semi-structured interviews with experts and smart building occupants
